Azure Waves Realty Property Management

Azure Waves Realty Inc provides full-service residential property management across San Diego County. We market your rental, screen and place tenants, collect rent, handle maintenance, and keep the books. The fee is 8% of collected monthly rent, plus half of the first month’s rent when we place a tenant. No upfront costs.

No two properties are the same, and neither are our management solutions. We craft individualized strategies for each property and client, aligning our services with your specific goals and the unique characteristics of your real estate asset. We ensure that every detail is meticulously handled.

For a competitive service fee of just 8% of the collected monthly rent (we don’t get paid unless you do), you can set aside the challenges of property management. Our services encompass everything from marketing, placing new tenants, end-to-end contract negotiations, 24-hour maintenance, meticulous bookkeeping, to efficient move-in and move-out procedures, and much more.

What does full-service management include?

Marketing & placing new tenants

Professional photos, listings on the top rental websites, and in-person showings 7 days a week.

End-to-end contract negotiations

A “Win-Win-Win” approach that eliminates gray areas before a lease is signed.

24-hour maintenance

A trusted network of vetted vendors, with a team ready to help 24/7.

Meticulous bookkeeping

Detailed monthly statements plus an annual summary that makes tax time easy.

Move-in & move-out procedures

Documented in writing and with photos, plus yearly walk-throughs of your home.

How much does full-service management cost?

Full-service management is 8% of collected monthly rent. The tenant placement fee is half of the first month’s rent. There are no upfront costs, and you pay no management fee during a vacancy or an eviction. If you only need a tenant, Tenant Placement Only is one month’s rent, with a $750 minimum. See pricing.

When do owners get paid?

Owner draws are paid by the 10th of every month. Tenants can pay rent online. You get a detailed monthly statement and an annual summary owner statement for tax time.

How fast is maintenance handled?

Urgent maintenance issues get a response within 24 hours. Non-urgent issues get a response within 48 hours. Our team handles emergencies 24/7 with vetted vendors.
