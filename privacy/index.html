Privacy Policy

Effective October 31, 2026. This page explains what Azure Waves Realty Inc collects through www.azwrealty.com, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information you give us

When you send a contact form, we receive your name, email, phone number, property address, the type of inquiry and your message. If you call or email us, we receive what you share in that call or email.

Information collected automatically

We use two analytics tools to understand how people use this site:

  • Google Analytics reports visits, pages viewed, how visitors found the site, device and browser type, and general location.
  • Microsoft Clarity records how visitors use pages, including clicks, scrolling and mouse movement. It uses this to create session recordings and heatmaps that we can review.

These tools use cookies or similar technology. Google and Microsoft handle the data they collect under their own terms and privacy policies.

This site also saves a small note in your browser about how you first arrived, such as the website that sent you. When you call, email or send a form from this site, we log that source and the page you were on. That log does not include your name, email, phone number or IP address.

How we use information

  • To answer your inquiry and provide our property management services
  • To see which pages help visitors and fix what does not work
  • To understand which marketing brings people to us
  • To keep the site secure and meet legal obligations

Who we share it with

We share information with service providers that help us run this site and respond to you, including our website, hosting, email and form providers, Google and Microsoft. We may also share information when the law requires it. We do not sell your personal information.

Your choices

  • You can block or clear cookies in your browser settings.
  • You can use Google’s Analytics opt-out browser add-on.
  • You can read Google’s privacy policy and Microsoft’s privacy statement.
  • You can ask us to review, correct or delete the personal information you sent us. Email contactusazw@gmail.com.

This site does not respond to browser Do Not Track signals.

Children

This site is for adults looking for property management services. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page and update the effective date.
