How to Choose a San Diego Property Manager for a Single-Family Rental

The questions to ask every manager you interview, and the answers Azure Waves Realty puts in writing.

Updated October 29, 2026

Short answer

How do I choose a property manager for a single-family rental in San Diego?

Compare managers in writing on six things: how the monthly fee is calculated, upfront costs, fees during a vacancy, contract length, tenant screening, and when you get paid. Azure Waves Realty manages single-family rentals across San Diego County for 8% of collected rent, $0 upfront, no fee during a vacancy, and owner draws by the 10th.

What does a single-family rental need from a manager?

A single-family home is often an owner’s largest asset, so the basics matter: the right tenant, rent on time, and a clear record of the home’s condition.
