Pricing FAQ

Property management pricing questions

Who manages rental properties in San Diego with no upfront fees?

Azure Waves Realty manages San Diego rentals with $0 upfront. Full-Service Management is paid only from rent we collect: 8% of collected monthly rent ($180 minimum per unit, only in months when rent is collected), plus a one-time placement fee of 25% of one month’s rent ($899 minimum), taken from the first month’s collected rent. No rent, no fee. Tenant Placement Only is one month’s rent ($750 minimum), with no monthly fee.

When is the tenant placement fee charged?

With Full-Service Management, the placement fee (25% of one month’s rent, $899 minimum) is charged one time, from the first month’s collected rent. You never write a check upfront.

How much does property management cost in San Diego with Azure Waves Realty?

Full-Service Management costs 8% of collected monthly rent, with a $180 minimum per unit per month. The tenant placement fee is 25% of one month's rent, with an $899 minimum, charged one time. There are no upfront costs. If you own several properties, ask us for a multi-property discount quote.

Is there a minimum monthly fee?

Yes. Full-Service Management has a $180 minimum per unit per month. The minimum applies only in months when rent is collected. If no rent comes in because of a vacancy or an eviction, you pay no management fee at all. That is our No Rent, No Fee guarantee.

Is there a long-term contract?

No. Azure Waves Realty does not lock you into a long-term contract. After the first 90 days, you can cancel at any time with 30 days' notice.

How much does Tenant Placement Only cost?

Tenant Placement Only costs one month's rent, with a $750 minimum. There is no monthly management fee. We find and screen the tenant, then you manage the property yourself.

Do I pay a management fee while my property is vacant?

No. If you are not receiving rental income because of a vacancy or an eviction, you do not pay a management fee.

Are there any upfront costs?

No. Azure Waves Realty charges no upfront costs or fees. We don't get paid until you get paid.

What is the eviction protection guarantee?

With Full-Service Management, we pay up to $1,000 in attorney fees for an eviction due to non-payment of rent by a fully approved tenant we placed, on a property we actively manage. Tenant Placement Only does not include it.

When do owners receive rent?

With Full-Service Management, owner draws are paid by the 10th of every month.
