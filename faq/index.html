San Diego Property Management FAQ

Plain answers about fees, leasing, maintenance, and owner reports.

Azure Waves Realty is a San Diego property management company. Full-service management costs 8% of collected monthly rent, plus a tenant placement fee of half the first month's rent. There are no upfront costs and no management fee during a vacancy or eviction. Below are answers to common owner questions.

  • Fees and payments
  • Leasing and tenants
  • Maintenance
  • Reports and communication
  • Getting started

Fees and payments

How much does property management cost with Azure Waves Realty?

Full-service management is 8% of collected monthly rent. The tenant placement fee is half of the first month's rent. There are no upfront costs. See the full breakdown on our Pricing page.

Do I pay a management fee if my property is vacant?

No. If you are not receiving rent because of a vacancy or eviction, you do not pay a management fee. Our contracts are structured so we make money only after you do. Read the details in Our Promise.

What does Tenant Placement Only cost?

Tenant Placement Only costs one month's rent, with a $750 minimum, and there is no monthly fee. It includes a rent assessment, marketing, professional photos, in-person showings, and tenant screening. You then manage the property yourself. Compare both plans on our Pricing page.

When do owners get paid?

Owner draws are paid by the 10th of every month by direct deposit. Tenants have multiple ways to pay, and we encourage online payments to speed up the process. Learn more about Rent Collection.

Leasing and tenants

How do you market a vacant rental?

We start with a free rental analysis, then take professional-grade photos. We list the property on top rental websites such as Zillow, Trulia, and HotPads, and we do in-person showings 7 days a week. Learn more about Marketing & Leasing.

How do you screen tenants?

Each application is checked for income, credit, eviction history, employment, criminal history, liens, and past rental references. Before the lease is signed, we remove gray areas so everyone is on the same page. Learn more about Tenant Screening & Leasing.

What happens if a tenant stops paying rent?

Our team has years of experience in tenant and landlord law and knows the proper notices to send. There is no management fee during an eviction. If a fully approved tenant we placed is evicted for non-payment of rent on a property we actively manage, we pay up to $1,000 in attorney fees. See Our Promise.

What if my property does not rent in the expected time?

We run a market rent analysis so your property is priced competitively when a lease begins or renews. If it is not rented within the expected time frame, we re-evaluate the pricing strategy and make adjustments at no extra charge. See Our Promise.

Maintenance

How are maintenance emergencies handled?

Our team is ready to help with emergencies 24/7. We guarantee a response to urgent maintenance issues within 24 hours. Learn more about Maintenance & Service.

How fast do you respond to non-urgent repairs?

We guarantee a response to non-urgent maintenance issues within 48 hours. Learn more about Maintenance & Service.

Who does the repair work?

We use vetted third-party vendors, from plumbers and electricians to contractors, gardeners, and handymen. Their work is guaranteed and warrantied. If you have a preferred vendor, we are happy to work with them. Learn more about Maintenance & Service.

Do you inspect the property?

Yes. We document move-ins and move-outs in writing and with photos. We also do yearly walk-throughs of your home. Learn more about Maintenance & Service.

Reports and communication

What reports do owners receive?

At the end of each month, you receive a detailed statement of each dollar coming in and going out. Each year, you receive an annual summary owner statement with all receipts, invoices, and statements. Learn more about Accounting.

Can I request reports or receipts at any time?

Yes. All reports, receipts, and tracking are available at all times. Email us a request and we will get back to you within 24 hours. Learn more about Accounting.

What if I feel out of the loop?

You get regular updates and detailed reports on your property, including financials, maintenance, and tenant issues. If you ever feel out of the loop, let us know and the Broker will reach out personally within 24 hours. See Our Promise.

Getting started

What areas do you serve?

We manage rentals across San Diego County, including San Diego, La Jolla, Pacific Beach, Carlsbad, Chula Vista, Escondido, Oceanside, El Cajon, and many more communities. Our commercial services expand nationwide. See Property Management for the full list.

What types of properties do you manage?

We manage single-family homes, condos, and multifamily properties. We also offer commercial leasing and advisory for property types such as office, retail, and industrial. Learn more about Property Management.

Can I switch from Tenant Placement Only to full-service management?

Yes. If you decide to move to full-service property management, our team is ready to support you. Compare both plans on our Pricing page.
