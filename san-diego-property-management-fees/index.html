San Diego Property Management Fees, Explained

Every fee a San Diego rental owner should ask about, what it pays for, and what Azure Waves Realty charges for each one.

Updated October 29, 2026

Short answer

Can I hire a San Diego property manager with no upfront fees?

Yes. Azure Waves Realty charges $0 upfront for San Diego rentals. Full-Service Management is 8% of collected monthly rent ($180 minimum per unit, only in months when rent is collected). The one-time placement fee, 25% of one month’s rent ($899 minimum), comes out of the first month’s collected rent. No rent, no fee.

Which plan fits my rental?

Choose Full-Service Management if you want one team to handle leasing, rent collection, maintenance, and accounting. Choose Tenant Placement Only if you want a screened tenant and plan to manage the property yourself. You can switch to full service later. Compare both plans.

Frequently asked questions

Are there any upfront costs with Azure Waves Realty?

No. Azure Waves Realty charges no upfront costs or fees on either plan.

When is the tenant placement fee charged?

With Full-Service Management, it is charged one time, from the first month’s collected rent.

Do I pay the $180 minimum when my rental is vacant?

No. The $180 minimum applies only in months when rent is collected. During a vacancy or an eviction, there is no management fee.

Is there a long-term contract?

No. After the first 90 days, you can cancel anytime with 30 days’ notice.
